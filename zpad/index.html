What you are hearing. The band is a 30-second excerpt of a song from the Slakh2100 test set, rendered from MIDI with sampled instruments; the model never saw it during training. The generated bass is produced causally, from audio that has already been heard, exactly as the live web app produces it, and is played with a simple additive synthesiser, so its timbre differs from the sampled bass of the "Real" line.

The pad. The model's two latent dimensions were learned without labels. For this model the horizontal axis mostly controls how busy the bass is; the lower-left corner moves it to a higher register. Each square is pre-rendered, so the pad jumps between 25 positions; the live app accepts any position and your own audio.
