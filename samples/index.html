Means over the group's clips. Beat-F: beats found by Beat This! in the bass versus in the band (±70 ms). COCOLA: how well the bass fits the band. “Mismatched” is the real bass of another clip, a floor. “Pitch class = real”: share of the time both basses sound in which the generated note has the real bass's pitch class. Twenty seconds per clip is short; treat single numbers as rough.

Songs the model was trained on: slim3 against B

An earlier comparison on 30 s of training songs (MUSDB18 and Slakh2100): the light model slim3 and the full model B, both fine-tuned on MUSDB18 + Slakh, against the real bass.

GTZAN has no stems: Demucs (htdemucs) splits each recording into bass and the rest; the model hears the rest and the separated bass is the reference. In RawStems the bass stem does not line up with the band (onset correlation 0.03 over 30 songs, against 0.25 to 0.35 for MUSDB18 and MoisesDB), so its “real” numbers are not a valid reference.
