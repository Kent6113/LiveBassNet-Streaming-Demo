LiveBassNet

Towards a live bass-accompaniment model

A causal, streaming version of BassNet: it listens to a band without a bass player and generates a bass line from the audio it has heard so far, with a 2-D control for the style of the bass. Research in progress.

  • LiveBassNet StudioLoad your own song without bass and generate a bass line in the browser, steering it live on the latent pad.
  • LiveBassNet PadDrag the latent z while three prepared songs play; the bass follows from the next frame on.
  • BassNet Z-PadListening demo: drag across the model's latent control pad and hear the generated bass line change over three songs.
  • BassNet on Slakh2100The causal BassNet trained on Slakh2100: data, test results, latent control and listening examples.
  • Streaming BassNetThe model running frame by frame: latency, the cost of strictly live decisions, and where the errors come from.
Based on BassNet: M. Grachten, S. Lattner, E. Deruty, “BassNet: A Variational Gated Autoencoder for Conditional Generation of Bass Guitar Tracks with Learned Interactive Control”, Applied Sciences 10(18), 2020. This is an independent reimplementation.
Audio excerpts from Slakh2100 (E. Manilow et al., WASPAA 2019), licensed CC BY 4.0; the generated bass lines are model output.
