LiveBassNet

A bass player that listens to your band, live

LiveBassNet hears a band without a bass player and plays a bass line from the music heard so far, with a beat tracker that runs alongside it. Everything runs in your browser. Try it, rate it, or take the listening test.

Try itStudio Load any song, or the example, and hear the generated bass. Steer its style on the pad while it plays, tap the beat if the tracker loses it, and rate the result. Take partListening test Ten 10-second excerpts, five bass lines each: rate how well each fits the band. About 15 minutes, headphones recommended. PasswordListening samples Clips from songs the model was trained on to commercial recordings it never saw, with the real bass for comparison. PasswordPrimer What the project does and why playing live is hard, in plain terms, for colleagues from other fields. PasswordReport The case for a paper against the closest work, and the full record of methods and results.

Based on BassNet: M. Grachten, S. Lattner, E. Deruty, “BassNet: A Variational Gated Autoencoder for Conditional Generation of Bass Guitar Tracks with Learned Interactive Control”, Applied Sciences 10(18), 2020. This is an independent reimplementation.
Audio excerpts in the studio and the listening test come from Slakh2100 (E. Manilow et al., WASPAA 2019), licensed CC BY 4.0; the listening test uses the test windows of Silent Metronome (Bretz et al., 2026). The generated bass lines are model output.
The studio's default models were also trained on MUSDB18 and MoisesDB (both for non-commercial research), and its beat tracker on beats estimated by Beat This!. Audio from these and other research datasets appears only on the password-protected pages, encrypted.
