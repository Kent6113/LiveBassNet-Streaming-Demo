How it works. BassNet's output at a frame depends only on the band audio up to that frame and on z; it never reads its own earlier output. The model's frame-by-frame output (pitch, level, onset) was therefore computed in advance on a 21 × 21 grid of z for each excerpt, and the page reads the grid point under the cursor at every frame, so what you hear is what the live model produces at that z (z is rounded to the nearest grid point, 0.3 apart). Notes are decided causally in the browser: a note starts one frame after the onset curve peaks above the threshold (the curve must first fall back below 0.6 × the threshold), and a simple four-harmonic synth plays it.
